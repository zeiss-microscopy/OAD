    Bases: Enum

    Enumerates possible job statuses.

    Attributes:

    • UNSPECIFIED –

      Default enum value.

    • NOT_LOADED –

      Job template is not loaded.

    • LOADED –

      Job template is loaded.

    • RUNNING –

      Job is running.

    • FINALIZING –

      Finalizing job results after the job was completed successfully.

    • ARCHIVING –

      Archiving job results after they were finalized.

    • COMPLETED –

      Job was completed successfully.

    • ABORTED –

      Job was aborted.

    • CANCELED –

      Job was canceled.

    Bases: ServiceStub

    Methods:

    • get_available_job_templates –

      Retrieves a list of all available job templates.

    • get_status –

      Gets the job status.

    • is_job_running –

      Checks if the loaded job is currently running.

    • is_job_template_loaded –

      Checks if a job template is loaded.

    • load_job_template –

      Loads a job template and prepares it for execution.

    • register_on_status_changed –

      Register on job status changed events.

    • run_job –

      Runs the loaded job template to completion.

    • start_job –

      Starts the loaded job template but it doesn't wait for it to complete.

    • stop_job –

      Stops the currently running job and waits for it to complete.

    • unload_job_template –

      Unloads the loaded job template.

    • wait_job –

      Waits for currently running job to complete.
